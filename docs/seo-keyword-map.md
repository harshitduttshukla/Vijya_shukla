## Cannibalization decisions

- H-frame, Cuplock and aluminium each use one comprehensive hybrid page. Separate `-mumbai` landing pages were not created because they would target the same products with substantially overlapping content.
- The homepage introduces every commercial service but links to the page with the more specific intent.
- `/scaffolding-material-parts` explains components; `/scaffolding-material-mumbai` handles supplier, rental and sale enquiries.
- `/scaffolding-guide` is a navigational hub. It does not compete with the definition article for “what is scaffolding.”

## Intentionally excluded keywords

The site does not target “what is scaffolding in teaching,” “scaffolding meaning in Hindi,” or “scaffolding meaning in Tamil.” Those searches have different intent or require genuinely useful language-specific content that the site does not currently provide.

## Internal linking model

1. Homepage links to commercial services, product guides and the knowledge hub.
2. The guide hub links to every informational guide and commercial service page.
3. Every article links to four related guides or services using descriptive, varied anchors.
4. Product cards link to their corresponding system guide.
5. Every page links to contact, telephone, WhatsApp and the core Mumbai services.

## Measurement

Track each page separately in Google Search Console. Compare queries, clicks, impressions, average position and conversions by intent. Do not merge pages solely because they share the word “scaffolding”; merge only when the user need and page purpose are the same.
