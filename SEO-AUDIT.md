**High impressions, low CTR (title and meta fix)**
- *8 types of scaffolding*: pos 3.2, 526 impressions, **1.7% CTR**. A top-3 result would normally get 8–15%. The title says "8 Systems Explained" but the searcher wants "8 types **with pictures**". Rewrite the title and meta to promise names and pictures. This is the biggest single quick win (an estimated 30–50 extra clicks a month).
- *what are the 8 types of scaffolding?*: pos 1.7, 0 clicks. Google likely answers this in a snippet. A clean numbered list helps you keep the snippet.

**Target keywords that are weak or have no page**
- *scaffolding manufacturer in mumbai*: pos 32.8, and there is no manufacturing content anywhere on the site. **Do you actually manufacture?** (See questions.)
- *aluminium scaffolding on rent in mumbai*: pos 25.4. /aluminium-scaffolding/ is mostly informational.
- *mobile scaffolding rental near me*, *scaffolding rental services*, *scaffolding rent*: no data and no dedicated commercial page.

**Extra secondary keywords from GSC (not on your list)**
- "scaffolding pictures with names", "scaffolding names", "scaffolding arrangement", "scaffolding system": these fit /types-of-scaffolding/
- "types of scaffolding materials": fits /scaffolding-material-parts/
- "h frame scaffolding on rent near me": fits /h-frame-scaffolding/ (rental section)
- Bengali "স্কাফোল্ডিং কত প্রকার" (17+9 impressions): a translated page would be possible, but the audience is outside Mumbai. **Skip it.**
- "types of scaffolding in construction" (pos 69–81): a different SERP. Add a "used in construction" H2 to the types page.

## 2. Tech stack and how pages are built

- **React 18 + Vite**, deployed on **Vercel** (`trailingSlash: true`).
- The homepage is built from components in [src/components/](src/components/). Its meta and schema are hard-coded in [index.html](index.html).
- The other 14 pages are data-driven: content, meta and FAQs live in [src/content/seoPages.js](src/content/seoPages.js) and are rendered by [src/components/SeoPage.jsx](src/components/SeoPage.jsx).
- **Static prerender (SSG)**: [scripts/prerender.mjs](scripts/prerender.mjs) writes full HTML for every route, so the HTML is good for SEO.
- The sitemap is auto-generated by [scripts/generate-sitemap.mjs](scripts/generate-sitemap.mjs), and [scripts/seo-audit.mjs](scripts/seo-audit.mjs) already validates titles, descriptions and schema (0 errors, 0 warnings).
- **There is no blog system.** New blog posts would fit easily into the existing `seoPages.js` data model, either as an "article" type or a new `/blog/` section.

## 5. Prioritised issues

### HIGH impact
1. **Cannibalisation between the homepage and /scaffolding-rental-mumbai/.** Both target "scaffolding rental Mumbai" (the existing [docs/seo-keyword-map.md](docs/seo-keyword-map.md) says so explicitly). The homepage should own "scaffolding (in) Mumbai" and the rental page should own every "rent/rental" phrase.
2. **"On rent" phrasing is missing.** No title or H1 contains "scaffolding on rent", yet this is how most Indian searchers phrase it (6 of your target queries).
3. **/types-of-scaffolding/ has low CTR.** 526 impressions at position 3.2 bring only 9 clicks. Rewrite the title and meta ("8 Types of Scaffolding with Pictures & Names"), add an image for each type (the page has only 1 image now), and expand it to about 1,500 words.
4. **There is no manufacturer content** for a target keyword (position 32.8).
5. **The LocalBusiness schema is incomplete.** It is missing `geo`, `openingHoursSpecification`, `priceRange` and `sameAs` (Google Business Profile, IndiaMART, Justdial and so on). It also uses `GeneralContractor`; the more precise type is `HomeAndConstructionBusiness`.

### MEDIUM impact
6. **Thin content on a money page.** /scaffolding-material-mumbai/ has 785 words. The product and guide pages are 780–950 words.
7. **Weak internal linking.** /aluminium-scaffolding/ (4), /mobile-scaffolding/ (3), /cantilever-scaffolding/ (3) and /suspended-scaffolding/ (3) get few inbound links. Aluminium and mobile matter because they map to rental keywords.
8. **Titles are too long.** 10 of 15 titles exceed 60 characters and get truncated, mostly because of the "| Shukla Scaffolding" suffix. Shorten the brand to "| Shukla" or drop it on the guide pages.
9. **There is no Google Map embed**, only a "search on Maps" link. An embed on the homepage and contact section helps local trust.
10. **There is no blog or content cluster** to support the rental pillar (rent price guide, rent vs buy, checklist, and so on).
11. **The Aluminium page H1 lacks rental/Mumbai intent**, while the title says "Mumbai Supply".

### LOW impact
12. One homepage image is missing alt text (likely decorative; I will verify).
13. The guide pages have a single hero image each. More real project photos would help "with pictures" queries and Image search.
14. `robots.txt` is fine. The sitemap has no `<lastmod>`-driven updates per page; this is minor.
15. The Bengali queries are not worth targeting.

### Already good (no action)
SSG/prerendered HTML, canonicals, OG tags, WebP and srcset images, sitemap, robots, a floating WhatsApp button, click-to-call (`tel:` links), consistent NAP (the Sakinaka address and 2 phone numbers are the same everywhere), FAQPage and Breadcrumb schema, and no broken links.

## 6. Questions before implementation

1. **Manufacturing:** do you actually manufacture scaffolding (a factory or fabrication unit)? If you only trade and supply, I will target "scaffolding supplier" instead of "manufacturer", because fake claims hurt trust.
2. **Aluminium and mobile towers:** do you rent aluminium mobile towers? If so, what heights are available?
3. **Products:** besides H-frame, MS H-frame, Cuplock, ladders and accessories, do you also offer MS pipes and clamps, props, shuttering plates or jacks?
4. **Rates:** can I publish a rough rent range (per day or month), or should I use "Get Quote" only?
5. **Business details:** opening hours, Google Business Profile URL, other listings (IndiaMART, Justdial), years in business, number of completed projects.
6. **Areas:** which of the 24 areas in the schema do you genuinely serve? Should location pages be made for 3–5 of them?
7. **GSC Pages tab:** a screenshot of it, so the ranking URLs can be confirmed.
